# Project Overview

**EarthTrend Detective** is a browser-based environmental research application built to investigate changes in Earth science observations, compare supported study regions and communicate the strength and limitations of trend evidence.

## Capabilities

- Explore a generalized interactive world map with country selection, zoom/pan and bounded geographic selections.
- Analyze a curated MERRA-2 temperature time series with charting and transparent methods.
- Analyze locally acquired NASA IMERG precipitation and GLDAS soil-moisture NetCDF collections through validated scientific pipelines.
- Explore approved research records, review scientific explanations grounded in observed statistics, and export research reports.
- Use light and dark themes, responsive navigation and accessible scientific summaries.

## Scientific boundaries

Curated source coverage and country boundaries are distinct. A selected country does not imply matching observational coverage. Spatial coverage, time periods, native units, completeness and dataset provenance must be validated before interpretation. Trend association is not causation.

## Technology

React, Vite, JavaScript, CSS, FastAPI, Python, xarray, NumPy, SciPy, Shapely, SQLite and Recharts.
